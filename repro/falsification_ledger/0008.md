## Live claim

Sparse routing attenuates raw expert gradients, but under adaptive optimizers that attenuation can largely cancel before the applied update. In the tested Moonlet `bf16` / `AdamW` lane, equal expert and dense learning rates are the right baseline and large multipliers like `15x` are overcorrections. The tested `nvfp4` / `ExpertAdamW` lane points in the same direction, but that precision story remains diagnostic.

## Formal object -> observable map

- routing attenuation: expert participation under normalized top-`k`
- raw signal: expert/dense `grad_to_param`
- applied step: expert/dense `optimizer_update_to_pre_param`
- regime dial: optimizer family, precision path, epsilon/noise floor, and LR multiplier

## Existing attacks

- two-seed `bf16` Moonlet multiplier sweep over `{0.5, 1, 2, 4, 15}`
- direct `bf16` update-proof runs at `1x` and `15x`
- `nvfp4` / `ExpertAdamW` update-proof runs at `1x` and `15x`
- short `nvfp4` grad-health canary

## Missing brutal attacks

- a non-adaptive control such as SGD, where the derivation predicts a different answer
- `ExpertMuon` or another adaptive-family control
- more seeds and more multipliers near the apparent boundary
- longer-horizon `nvfp4` runs that separate early stability from later quality
- an explicitly epsilon- or noise-dominated regime designed to break cancellation

## Launch gate

- Green: the post claims exactly the optimizer- and precision-bounded result that survived the current attacks: `bf16` is settled, `nvfp4` is directional, and no universal law is claimed.
- Red: any universal statement about MoE expert LR, or any claim that the full `nvfp4` optimum is closed, without the missing counter-regime attacks.
