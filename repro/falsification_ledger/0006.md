## Live claim

At extreme sparsity, loss can keep improving while the effective expert system collapses. The system is still learning, but it is learning with a much smaller active expert set than the config advertises.

## Formal object -> observable map

- starvation pressure: tokens per routed expert
- collapse observables: `max_load`, entropy, `CV`, effective experts
- temporal structure: early-layer-first propagation of collapse

## Existing attacks

- the post shows the strongest basic counterexample: smooth loss with broken routing
- `max_load` pins near `1/K`
- entropy and `CV` degrade materially
- the layerwise propagation plot attacks the idea that this is only a late-layer or terminal artifact

## Missing brutal attacks

- an `E` sweep with more intermediate points between healthy and Super-4096
- more seeds to separate deterministic attractor from fragile one-off failure
- rescue interventions to test whether the collapse is avoidable or structural in this token regime
- matched-token and matched-tokens-per-expert controls

## Launch gate

- Green: ship as a concrete collapse-mode paper, not a universal theorem of ultra-sparse MoE
- Red: claiming the precise attractor mechanism is closed without the rescue / intermediate-point studies
