## Live claim

Bounded config-only autoresearch is already useful on the public `nmoe` surface if the controller is constrained by an explicit mutation surface, a fixed budget, and an eval-gated promotion rule.

## Formal object -> observable map

- search space: allowlisted config fields only
- budget: fixed `512`-step candidate benchmark
- decision rule: primary metric plus `CORE` veto
- parallel semantics: local-wave keep/discard vs global champion progression

## Existing attacks

- the controller is forced to reject two better-looking validation points because the eval gate says no
- the winning path is reconstructed from raw campaign receipts
- the first parallel wave attacks the idea that this was just one lucky local tweak

## Missing brutal attacks

- comparison against a deterministic fallback search policy on the same budget
- repeat campaigns on additional public lanes, not just one canonical super-fp8 lane
- code-editing or broader-mutation surfaces, which are explicitly out of scope here

## Launch gate

- Green: the post stays scoped to one real config-only campaign with a real eval veto and a real kept winner
- Red: any implication that general autonomous research is solved by the current campaign surface
