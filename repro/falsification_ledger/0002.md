## Live claim

Loss is not a sufficient statistic for MoE health. A usable MoE measurement contract needs:

- one smooth optimization signal (`bpb`)
- one capability signal (`CORE`)
- one routed-model health surface (`CV`, entropy, `max_load`, dead experts)

## Formal object -> observable map

- compression: `bpb = loss * tokens / (bytes * ln 2)`
- capability: centered task accuracy / `CORE`
- health: router balance and concentration metrics
- distributed correctness: live lockstep eval for sharded MoE models

## Existing attacks

- healthy-vs-collapsed router comparison is in the receipt bundle
- Super-4096 gives the concrete counterexample where loss improves while routing breaks
- the post also documents the MoE distributed-eval gotcha and the live-lockstep guard

## Missing brutal attacks

- a false-positive study where router telemetry looks healthy but capability is broken
- a false-negative study where one router metric is noisy but the model remains healthy
- a seed study showing that the three-surface contract is more predictive than loss alone

## Launch gate

- Green: definitions are explicit, at least one sharp counterexample exists, and the wrong eval path is ruled out explicitly
- Red: presenting the metric stack as a proven minimal basis without the adversarial cases above
