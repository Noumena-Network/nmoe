## Live claim

Small-ish frontier MoE training difficulty is not one problem. It is the coupling of three problems:

- sparse expert-batch fragmentation and cold expert compute
- unstable or undertrained routing dynamics
- data quality / coverage constraints that matter more once compute is already sparse

## Formal object -> observable map

- fragmentation: `mu ≈ T * K / E` and grouped-GEMM utilization
- routing health: `CV`, entropy, `max_load`, expert activity
- system pressure: dispatch / compute / return timing surfaces
